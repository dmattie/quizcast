# While a risky outcome is still pending, which region tracks the *uncertainty* of that outcome rather than its expected value?

- [ ] Primary visual cortex
- [x] Anterior insula
- [ ] Cerebellar cortex
- [ ] Broca's area

> Anterior insula activity scales with risk during the anticipation window,
> separably from the striatal signal that scales with expected value
> (Preuschoff, Quartz & Bossaerts, 2008). Value and uncertainty are carried on
> different channels, not one combined signal.
