# Which regions are consistently implicated in subjective valuation during economic choice? Select all that apply.

Several answers are correct. Take your time before locking in.

- [x] Ventromedial prefrontal cortex
- [x] Ventral striatum
- [ ] Primary auditory cortex
- [x] Orbitofrontal cortex
- [ ] Cerebellar vermis

> vmPFC, ventral striatum and OFC recur across subjective-value meta-analyses
> (Bartra, McGuire & Kable, 2013). Auditory cortex and cerebellar vermis are
> the distractors.
